# Design Direction — Portfolio & CV: Võ Nguyên Hào

## 1. Identity & Character
- **Role:** BOD Sales Assistant | IT Pre-Sales & Enterprise Solution Consultant.
- **Industry & Domain:** Enterprise B2B Solutions, ERP, Supply Chain / Logistics, Banking Integrations, Security Compliance (ISO 27001).
- **Tone:** Corporate, Authoritative, Rigorous, Crisp, Professional. Not an "AI developer hobbyist", not playful neon tech.

## 2. Dials (Antislop Liveliness)
- **ENERGY:** 2 (Professional corporate rigor, clear hierarchy, focused contrast).
- **RHYTHM:** 2 (Structured grid with purposeful density variation: compact data sidebar, expanded project breakdowns, clean timeline).
- **MOTION:** 1 (Subtle, purposeful transitions: focus ring, hover elevation, smooth tab switching. Zero floating blobs, zero bouncy templates).

## 3. Visual System & Palette (Strict max 3 colors + 1 accent)
- **Backgrounds:** 
  - Primary Surface: Slate Clean `#0f172a` (Corporate Navy) / `#ffffff` (Pure Crisp White on documents).
  - Subtle Surface: `#f8fafc` / `#1e293b`.
- **Text & Borders:**
  - High Contrast Dark: `#0f172a` (9:1+ contrast on light), `#f8fafc` (12:1+ on dark).
  - Subtle Borders: `#e2e8f0` (light) / `#334155` (dark).
- **Brand Identity Accent:**
  - Steel Blue `#0284c7` (Purposeful accent for technical integrity and active links).
  - Security / Verified Emerald `#059669` (Strictly reserved for ISO 27001 verified badges & active status).
- **Banned:**
  - ❌ Floating glowing blur blobs (`.hero-blob`).
  - ❌ Artificial blueprint/matrix grid lines (`background-size: 60px 60px`).
  - ❌ Em dashes (`—`) in UI copy (replaced by `,`, `:`, or `(`).
  - ❌ Generic AI buzzwords ("Seamless", "Revolutionary", "Cutting Edge").
  - ❌ Fake stats or pill capsules parked above H1 with blinking dots.

## 4. Typography
- **Primary:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` — Crisp modern geometric sans with high legibility.
- **Data / Codes:** `JetBrains Mono` — Strictly for IDs, API endpoints, technical protocol labels.
